Summary

The Dark Knight follows Batman as he tries to protect Gotham City from the Joker, a criminal mastermind who creates chaos throughout the city. With the help of Lieutenant James Gordon and District Attorney Harvey Dent, Batman faces one of his greatest challenges while discovering the true meaning of justice and sacrifice.
